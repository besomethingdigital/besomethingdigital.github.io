FAQ

Common questions

Do you offer same-day plumbing service?

When the schedule allows, yes. Call early for the best chance of a same-day visit, especially for active leaks or sewer backups.

How do you price jobs?

We diagnose first, explain options, and give a clear estimate before paid work begins. No surprise invoices after the fact.

Can you replace a water heater?

Yes — tank and many tankless models. We’ll confirm fit, venting, and permit needs before install day.

What if my drain keeps clogging?

Repeated clogs often mean a deeper line issue. We can clear the drain and, when useful, camera-inspect so you know what’s really going on.
